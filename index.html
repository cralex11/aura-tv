Pin Launcher to TV Home

1. Press Home on your TV remote to go to the native menu.

2. Scroll to Clean Launcher in your Apps row.

3. Hold down OK for 2 seconds (or press the Edit / Options ☰ button).

4. Press the Left Arrow to slide it to the very first spot (#1).

5. Press OK to lock it. In TV Settings → System → Advanced → Power On Mode, select "Remember Last App" so it boots straight here!
